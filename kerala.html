KSR & Kerala Service Forms Generator

Leave applications, insurance & accident-benefit forms, transfer of charge, advances, and more — with signature capture & live preview.

Before you rely on this: this tool drafts your application in the standard KSR/KFC formats so you have a clean starting point — it is not an official Government of Kerala product and is not legal advice. Rule numbers, appendices, and required enclosures are amended from time to time by Government Orders, and offices sometimes have local variations. Please confirm current requirements — and whether your office needs a wet-ink signature rather than a printed one — with your DDO / Head of Office before submission. GPF and GIS are deliberately not included here: both are now handled through dedicated online modules (SPARK for GPF, the "Viswas" portal for GIS) where a manual paper application is generally no longer accepted.
